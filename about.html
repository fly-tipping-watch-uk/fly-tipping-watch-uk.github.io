About the project

An independent public-information and accountability tool

Fly-Tipping Watch UK helps people find official routes, understand published evidence and document public-body responses.

What the site does

It brings together nation-specific reporting guidance, official statistics, complaint routes, information-rights guidance and documented public outcomes.

The site is maintained independently on a voluntary basis. It is not operated by a council, regulator, government department or ombudsman.

What the site does not do

  • It does not submit an official report for you.
  • It does not investigate suspected offenders.
  • It does not publish unmoderated allegations.
  • It does not replace legal advice or an emergency service.

Review cycle

How the information is maintained

Official annual statistics are reviewed when new releases appear. Reporting and complaint links are checked periodically and when an error is reported.
