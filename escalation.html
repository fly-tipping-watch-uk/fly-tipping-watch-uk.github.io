Escalation planner

Identify the next proportionate step

A service report and a complaint are not the same thing. Build a dated record before increasing pressure.

The accountability ladder

Escalate the handling, not merely the frustration

Not every case needs every stage. Use the lowest stage capable of producing the required action.

1

Official service report

Use the correct route and retain the reference and stated target.

2

Evidence-based follow-up

State what remains unresolved and ask for a specific action date.

3

Formal complaint

Complain separately about delay, communication or failure to follow process.

4

Elected representatives and scrutiny

Provide a concise chronology, unresolved point and requested outcome.

5

Environmental information request

Ask for existing recorded policy, targets, data, decisions or resource information.

6

Ombudsman review

Use the appropriate national route after completing the public body's process where required.

7

Verified publication

Publish the chronology, source documents, response and eventual outcome.

Information rights

Ask for records, not explanations that do not exist

Environmental Information Regulations requests normally require a response as soon as possible and no later than 20 working days, subject to the applicable rules and possible extensions for complex, voluminous requests.

Useful recorded information

  • Current investigation and clearance policy
  • Published or internal service targets
  • Monthly incident and enforcement data
  • Hotspot criteria and intervention records
  • Camera deployment policy
  • Recorded reasons for taking or declining action
