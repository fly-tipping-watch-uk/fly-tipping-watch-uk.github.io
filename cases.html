Published ombudsman finding

Delayed response to a fly-tipping complaint

Caerphilly County Borough Council · Wales · 4 August 2021

Early resolution

The Public Services Ombudsman for Wales recorded concern that the complainant had not received a response and had been inconvenienced by the council's actions.

Recorded outcome

The council agreed to apologise for the delay and provide a complaint response. The recommendations were actioned before the Ombudsman's enquiries concluded.

Practical lessons

Keep the original service report separate from the complaint about how it was handled.
Record dates and unanswered correspondence.
Complete the authority's complaint process before approaching the ombudsman unless exceptional circumstances apply.
