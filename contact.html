Do not include personal or sensitive material

GitHub issues are public. Do not post names of suspected offenders, private email addresses, telephone numbers, exact residential details, faces, registration plates, evidence files or unpublished correspondence.

Public-body correction or right of reply

A public body may use the factual correction form for material that can be stated publicly. The submission should identify the page, the precise point said to be inaccurate and an official source supporting the correction.

A private evidence-submission and correspondence route will be established before the site begins accepting live case submissions. Until then, the site does not invite or collect private case material.
