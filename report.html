Official route finder

Report the incident to the body that can act

The route depends on the UK nation, the scale or risk of the waste and who controls the land. This tool links to official services; it does not submit the official report for you.

Official routes by nation

England

Ordinary incidents normally go to the relevant council. Serious environmental incidents may involve the Environment Agency.

Wales

Ordinary incidents normally go to the relevant local authority; serious environmental incidents may involve Natural Resources Wales.

Open official guidance

Scotland

Report ordinary incidents to the relevant council. Hazardous or water-related events may involve SEPA or Police Scotland.

Northern Ireland

Report ordinary incidents to the council. Serious and persistent waste crime may involve the Northern Ireland Environment Agency.
