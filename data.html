How to use council data fairly

  • Show the reporting year and source date.
  • Use population rates where available, not totals alone.
  • Publish reporting-basis changes alongside the number.
  • Do not treat more recorded reports as proof of worse service.
  • Separate clearance performance from enforcement activity.
