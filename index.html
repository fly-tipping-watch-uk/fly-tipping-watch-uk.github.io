Three separate records

Do not let the issue become blurred

A strong case distinguishes the incident, the service request and any complaint about handling.

01

The incident

What was observed, where, when, the type and scale of waste, and the evidence retained.

02

The service report

Which body received it, the official reference, acknowledgement, target and action promised.

03

The handling complaint
